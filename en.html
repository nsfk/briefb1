  1. Choose a time, then press Start. When time is up, the app rings and shows a message.
  2. Write on the lined sheet. The sheet grows with your text; the ä ö ü ß bar stays right under the clock when you scroll.
  3. Press Grade to see the spelling mistakes, the score and the comparison with the model answer.
  4. To practise copying letter by letter, choose Tracing — the model answer shows faintly and you type over it; wrong capitals, punctuation and umlauts/ß are coloured at once.
  5. To focus on writing, press the expand button in the right corner of the „Your text“ box. The task and the model answer are hidden, the sheet becomes wider and the text larger; press the button again to go back.
  6. Have a good task and model answer? Press Contribute in the „Model answer“ box to share them with everyone. The model answer needs more than 50 words, more than 10 sentences and a spelling score of at least 90; once the site owner has reviewed it, it appears under „Sample tasks…“.

Đang mở

Chỉ chủ trang hoặc người có quyền chỉnh sửa mới mở hoặc tạm đóng trang được.

  1. Mở kho GitHub của trang, vào thư mục có index.html.
  2. Bấm vào tệp site.json → biểu tượng bút chì (Edit) → dán nội dung trên thay cho nội dung cũ. Hoặc chọn Add file → Upload files và tải lên tệp vừa lưu.
  3. Press Commit changes. Sau khoảng 1–2 phút mọi người mở trang sẽ thấy thay đổi; trang đang mở tự kiểm tra lại mỗi phút.

Names and words that are not in the dictionary. These words are not counted as mistakes. Names in the task, in the greeting at the top of the letter and in the signature at the end are skipped automatically.

While writing Schreiben

By default the page blocks automatic translation, Grammarly, LanguageTool, writing suggestions and the phone keyboard's automatic capitals in the writing boxes, because they often cover the cursor, suggest the wrong language or correct mistakes for you. The tracing box is always blocked. Some AI extensions show a button when you select text; the page cannot block them, so switch them off for this page in the extension's settings.

The Windows file picker can take a few seconds to appear, and the browser stands still meanwhile. Your text is always saved before the picker opens. Faster: drag a file into the task / model answer box, or paste the content.

Data in this browser Daten

Drafts, history and settings are stored only in this browser. Your texts are not sent anywhere.

The page's server only counts page openings, gradings and tracing sessions and the dictionary loading time; it does not receive your texts, names or IP addresses. On the public page (GitHub Pages) this option also turns on Google Tag Manager, Google's statistics service; Google may use cookies and IP addresses under Google's policy. Switching this option off stops both (Google Tag Manager from the next page load).

Backup to a file

Data in the browser can be lost when the cache is cleared or you change computers. The backup file (.json) contains the grading and tracing history, the settings, „My words“ and the draft.

About Über

Spelling is checked with Hunspell 1.7.3 (WebAssembly) and the de_DE dictionary igerman98 © Björn Jacke (GPL 2/3), plus a list of newer words (App, Homeoffice, vietnamesisch…). The app runs entirely in the browser and needs no internet connection.

The score reflects spelling only (Rechtschreibung), including capitals at the start of a sentence and nouns written in lower case. Grammar and content are not graded. In the telc exam, the examiners also assess whether all points of the task (Leitpunkte) are covered, the layout of the letter and the accuracy of the language.

B1 Schreibtrainer helps you practise writing German letters at level B1 (telc, Goethe, ÖSD): exam timer, spelling check, comparison with a model answer and tracing practice, right in the browser. The score reflects spelling only; grammar and content are not graded.
